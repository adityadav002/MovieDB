Chronicles of Eden

2026 2h 34m PG-13

In a distant future where Earth is uninhabitable, a lone explorer discovers a forgotten paradise planet. But as they delve deeper into its lush jungles, they realize they are not alone. A visual masterpiece of survival and discovery.

info Overview

In the year 2026, Earth has become a silent tomb of steel and glass. Commander Elias Thorne, a veteran explorer, embarks on a desperate mission to the edge of the known galaxy following a faint signal from a long-lost probe.

What he finds is Eden—a world of impossible beauty and terrifying secrets. As Elias and his crew navigate the bioluminescent jungles, they discover that the planet's ecosystem is a sentient network that has been waiting for humanity's return. Chronicles of Eden is a profound exploration of survival, legacy, and the cost of a second chance.
